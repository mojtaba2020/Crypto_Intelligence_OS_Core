# Crypto Intelligence OS
## Prediction Ledger Schema v0.1

CONFIDENTIAL — INTERNAL USE ONLY

Version: 0.1

Purpose:
Create a tamper-resistant historical record of Human, AI, and Hybrid predictions
before market outcomes are known.

---

# 1. Prime Rule

A prediction must be recorded BEFORE its outcome is known.

Once a prediction is locked:

- It must never be silently edited
- It must never be deleted because it was wrong
- Confidence must not be changed afterward
- Reasoning must not be rewritten afterward
- Entry or target levels must not be adjusted afterward

If a prediction changes:

Create a NEW prediction.

Never rewrite history.

---

# 2. Prediction Sources

Every forecast must identify its source.

Allowed source types:

HUMAN
AI
HYBRID

## HUMAN

Independent Mojtaba forecast.

The Human forecast should be recorded before seeing the final AI forecast
when running Human-vs-AI experiments.

## AI

Independent machine forecast.

The AI should not see Mojtaba's final prediction during independent evaluation.

## HYBRID

Generated only after Human and AI independent tracks exist.

Hybrid may combine:

- Human market expertise
- Quantitative evidence
- AI analysis
- On-chain evidence
- Market regime
- Technical evidence
- Cycle evidence
- Narrative evidence
- Risk evidence

---

# 3. Unique Prediction ID

Every prediction receives a permanent unique identifier.

Format example:

PRED-2026-000001

Future format may include:

PRED-BTC-2026-000001

IDs must never be reused.

# 5. Timestamp Standard

UTC should be the canonical timestamp.

Example:

2026-09-13T04:25:31Z

Local display times may be stored additionally.

All evidence used for a prediction must have been available before:

data_cutoff_time

No future evidence may enter the forecast.

BEARISH
NEUTRAL

UP

---

# 9. Confidence

Confidence should be numeric.

Recommended scale:

0–100

Example:

72

Meaning:

The forecasting source estimates approximately 72% confidence in the defined prediction.

Confidence is NOT a measure of enthusiasm.

It is a measurable probability-like forecast that should later be calibrated.

---

# 10. Market Regime

Record the market environment at prediction time.

Possible initial values:

BULL
BEAR
SIDEWAYS
TRANSITION
HIGH_VOLATILITY
RISK_ON
RISK_OFF
UNKNOWN

Regime definitions must eventually be generated by a versioned Market Regime Engine.

---

# 11. Thesis

Every prediction must include a concise thesis.

Example:

"BTC remains above long-term trend support while liquidity conditions
and market breadth are improving."

The thesis should explain WHY the prediction exists.

---

# 12. Evidence

Store evidence separately from conclusion.

Examples:

Technical Evidence
On-Chain Evidence
Cycle Evidence
Liquidity Evidence
Narrative Evidence
Macro Evidence
Tokenomics Evidence

Evidence should include source references whenever possible.

---

# 13. Counterarguments

Every serious prediction must include evidence against itself.

Example:

- Falling spot volume
- Negative exchange flows
- Weak market breadth
- Regulatory risk

No prediction should contain only supporting evidence.

---

# 14. Known Unknowns

The system should explicitly record missing information.

Examples:

- incomplete wallet data
- delayed token unlock data
- unknown OTC activity
- uncertain regulatory event

Unknown information must not be silently treated as certainty.

---

# 17. Locking State

Prediction lifecycle:

DRAFT
↓
REVIEWED
↓
LOCKED
↓
MATURED
↓
EVALUATED
↓
ARCHIVED

Only DRAFT predictions may be freely edited.

After LOCKED:

Core prediction fields become immutable.

---

# 18. Amendment Rule

If a locked prediction needs correction:

Do NOT edit it.

Create an amendment record.

Example:

PRED-2026-000122

superseded_by:

PRED-2026-000129

The original remains permanently available.

# 21. Confidence Calibration

Predictions should be grouped by confidence.

Example:

Confidence 50–59
Actual success = 54%

Confidence 60–69
Actual success = 65%

Confidence 70–79
Actual success = 73%

Confidence 80–89
Actual success = 61%

This reveals overconfidence or underconfidence.

# 23. Independence Rule

During controlled Human-vs-AI experiments:

Human must not see AI final forecast.

AI must not see Human final forecast.

Hybrid is created only afterward.

This prevents information contamination.

# 25. No Cherry-Picking

ALL qualifying predictions must remain in the ledger.

Do not publish only winners.

Store:

Wins
Losses
Neutral outcomes
Invalid predictions
Missing-data failures

Failure history is strategic data.

---

# 26. Invalid Prediction State

A prediction may be marked INVALID only for predefined reasons.

Examples:

DATA_FAILURE
EXCHANGE_OUTAGE
INCORRECT_ASSET_MAPPING
CORRUPTED_INPUT
SYSTEM_FAILURE

Bad market performance is NOT a valid reason to invalidate a prediction.

# 30. Human Approval

High-impact predictions may require explicit human approval before LOCKED state.

Example:

AI Prediction
↓
Review
↓
Mojtaba Approval
↓
LOCK

Approval does not mean Mojtaba agrees with the prediction.

It confirms the prediction record is ready to be frozen.

---

# 31. Prediction Quality Checklist

Before locking:

Is the asset defined?

Is the time horizon defined?

Is the direction defined?

Is confidence recorded?

Is invalidation defined?

Is evidence recorded?

Are counterarguments recorded?

Is the data cutoff recorded?

Are model and agent versions recorded?

Is the prediction independent where required?

If not:

Do not lock.

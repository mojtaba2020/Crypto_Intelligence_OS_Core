## Stage-6 scientific program exit criteria (all required)

1. **Provenance**: immutable artifact fingerprints, exchange/source coverage,
   precise origin as-of/maturity, closed candle availability, completeness, and
   an explicit immutable locked period.
2. **No leakage**: no center/future features, cross-fold fitted transforms,
   immature-label training, selection/diagnostic target overlap, or use of V2
   locked-OOS outcomes to tune V3.
3. **Prespecified comparison**: persistence baseline read-only; candidate
   family, feature masks, horizons, loss, folds, purge, dependence-aware
   bootstrap, minimum origins, alpha, full multiplicity family, and
   adversarial checks fixed **before any new independent diagnostic outcome**.
4. **Evidence**: enough mature chronological origins for legitimate
   inference, positive mean and lower bound, corrected Holm rejection, and
   the complete declared robustness/falsification packet. A positive mean,
   successful CI, or missing sensitivity is not a pass.
5. **Governance**: independent review and manual freeze decision; no
   automatic champion, Fresh Locked OOS, or production promotion.

# First Paying Customer — 14-day evidence gate (v0.1)

Status: HYPOTHESIS, not validated demand or a revenue forecast. Owner: founder. Date: 2026-09-19.

## One initial customer hypothesis

Independent crypto research analysts and small research teams who regularly prepare source-backed BTC market briefs may pay for an auditable, time-stamped BTC/USD data-quality and research briefing workflow. **Do not assume this is true.** Individual retail traders, funds, and exchanges are different customer groups; do not blend their needs or claim this product predicts prices.

## Pain to test, not to assert

How much time and money do prospective customers actually spend gathering, checking, documenting and reproducing BTC market evidence? Which existing tools do they use, what is still difficult, and who can approve a purchase?

## Current asset / missing product

Already verified: private Coinbase BTC/USD daily ingestion and durable archive, 90 consecutive daily candles at last verified run, source-integrity inspection, read-only GitHub Actions missions, private downloadable static HTML console. This is **internal infrastructure**, not a live hosted customer product, AI agent, trading engine, profitable strategy or paying customer.

Missing: interviews, consented design partners, customer-facing workflow, quantified value, pricing evidence, security/privacy requirements and distribution channel.

## Discovery protocol — no speculative engineering until evidence

1. Recruit 10 people who **actually produce BTC research**; record role, team size, current workflow, tools, frequency, current spending, procurement authority and permission to follow up. Avoid only friends who would give polite encouragement.
2. Ask for the most recent real research task, its inputs, validation steps, time taken, errors and consequences. Ask them to show a redacted example if willing. Do not pitch the solution first or ask leading questions such as 'Would you buy my AI OS?'.
3. Identify a repeated, costly problem across at least 5 of the 10 interviews. Record contrary evidence and existing alternatives. If not observed, revise the customer/problem hypothesis rather than adding features.
4. Offer a **manual, read-only, source-cited BTC evidence brief** to 3 consenting design partners, with explicit as-of timestamp, data lineage, missing-data flags and reproducibility notes. No investment recommendations, guaranteed signals, account connections, autonomous agents or trades.
5. Ask for a concrete paid pilot or a documented purchasing process, not just praise or signups. Price, scope, security requirements and buyer must come from conversations; do not invent willingness to pay.

## Go / revise decision

Proceed to build the smallest repeatable brief workflow only if: (a) >=5/10 report the same recurring costly job, (b) >=3 partners agree to test a real brief, and (c) >=1 authorized buyer agrees to a paid pilot or provides a concrete dated procurement step. These are **internal experiment thresholds**, not market facts. If any fail, review interview evidence and change the hypothesis; do not build an AI agent or another dashboard as a substitute.

## Minimal product contract, only after gate

Input: auditable BTC/USD public market data, source and timestamp. Output: reproducible evidence brief with data coverage, anomalies, comparisons, source citations, caveats and human review. Success metric: measured minutes saved per brief and willingness to pay; guardrails: no fabricated facts, no price prediction promises, no trade execution, no unauthorized access or publishing private research.

## Model selection rule

No model vendor or version is preselected. First define the benchmark from consented customer tasks, then evaluate current available models for factuality, provenance, latency, cost, privacy/data retention and reliability against a deterministic baseline. Keep provider adapters replaceable and version-pin evaluated deployments. Newest is not automatically best.

## CEO weekly evidence dashboard (manual until needed)

Record: interviews completed / 10; common costly jobs / 10; design partners / 3; paid-pilot commitments / 1; measured brief preparation time before/after; cash received; open risks. All start at **0 / unmeasured** until founder supplies real evidence. Do not represent interview or revenue targets as achieved.
